Control panel — layout redesign

Three problems from the screenshot: the footer holds eight controls and drops Stop and Quit at normal width, the status hero eats roughly a third of the window, and everyday settings like Start at login sit below the fold. The aim is fewer things competing for attention, not more screens.

Everything moved out of the footer is either rare or destructive, and neither belongs next to the primary action.

WasNowWhy
Load / Delete / Save as + profile box⋯ → Profiles Four controls for something used once a month, permanently occupying the busiest row.
Restore defaults⋯ → Restore defaults Destructive and rare. Sitting beside Save invites the misclick that reset your config.
Quit⋯ → Quit The window ✕ already quits, so the button was a duplicate of a control that never disappears.
Start + StopOne Start/Stop sharing button They are mutually exclusive; one is always greyed. A single toggle halves the row and always reads as the thing you can do.
Theme swatches in the header⋯ → Appearance Six colour dots at the top of every screen, next to the one status you actually read.

Footer goes from 8 controls to 2, so Stop can no longer be pushed off the edge at normal width — the bug visible in the screenshot.

The startup group is the one you said you have to scroll for. It is its own concern — how the app behaves at login — and shares nothing with picking a peer address, so splitting it removes the scroll rather than just reordering it.

4. Open questions

#QuestionMy suggestion
Q1Keep Clipboard as its own tab, or fold it into Security? Fold it in — four tabs read more calmly than five, and clipboard is two controls.
Q2Should the compact hero stay visible on every tab, or only when connected? Always visible. It is the one thing that answers "where is my keyboard right now".
Q3Window size — keep 600×840? Narrower and shorter is now possible: ~560×720 with no scrolling on any tab.
